Twiglit
product

The collaborative task & project management app.

twig
noun · atom

A single item in the tree — task, note, project, any unit of work.

twiglits
noun · children

The children of a twig — subtasks, anything nested.

tree
noun · whole

The full hierarchical structure of twigs in a workspace.

berry
noun · bullet

The small circle on the left of each twig. Tap to focus.

leaf
noun · detail

The detail panel on the right — metadata for the selected twig.

focus mode
verb · zoom

Zoom into a single twig + its twiglits, nothing else.

twigler
noun · person

A Twiglit user.

shared twig
noun · green berry

A twig shared with other twiglers. Shown with a green berry.

multiplied twig
noun · purple berry

A twig mirrored in multiple places in the tree.

workspace
noun · root

The root container for a twigler's entire tree. Hidden from UI.

owner · assignee · participant
noun · roles

Created the twig · responsible for it · follower with comment access.
