Section · Insight 03 / 08

Master · Two column

A new tool for an old habit.

Today

Knowledge lives in chat logs.

Twenty open tabs. Three AI accounts. A folder of screenshots. Anything worth keeping gets copy‑pasted into Notion at midnight, or — more often — forgotten by Friday.

With Twiglit

Knowledge lives in a tree.

Every twig is a thought. Twigl in what matters from any AI or person, branch it, share branches with the people who need them. The next AI you talk to reads the tree and picks up where the last one left off.

Section · Solution 04 / 08

Master · Three pillars

What Twiglit is

Three principles, one tree.

01

Trees, not folders.

Every twig has a parent. Move one, the children move with it. The structure of your thinking is the structure of the file.

02

Berries, not checkboxes.

One bullet says it all — empty, focused, shared, multiplied. The berry is the bullet, the status, and the brand.

03

Calm, never quiet.

Sub‑100ms motion. No notifications. The tree is patient because the work is. Twiglit waits for you, not the other way around.
