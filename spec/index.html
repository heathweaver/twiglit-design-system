Principles

How Twiglit feels

Four principles for when the rules don't decide for you.

01

Calm, never quiet

Twiglit is patient with you, but it isn't passive. Movement is gentle; copy is direct. No spinners that just spin — every wait teaches you something.

02

Tree before time

Information is organized by relationship first, schedule second. Lists nest. Views on the same data share state, never duplicate it.

03

The berry knows

One bullet, one status, one source of truth. Completion, ownership, and progress live on the row itself — never in a separate column you forget to update.

04

Grow into the AI

The grain (skill) and the propose-tray are first-class. Surfaces always make it obvious where the human ends and the assistant begins, and reversal is one tap.
