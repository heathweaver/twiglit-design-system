01 · Action

Buttons

Four intent levels — primary, secondary, ghost, danger. No pill rounding. One primary per surface.

Variants

.btn-primary · .btn-secondary · .btn-ghost · .btn-danger

Sizes

.btn-sm · default · .btn-lg

With icons

always label — never icon-only when actionable
ANATOMY
height 30 (default) · 38 (large) · 22 (small)
padding 14×7 · gap 8 · radius 0
font-size --t-small · weight 600 · letter-spacing -0.015em
✓ Do

One primary per surface. Action-first verbs: "Save", "Add", "Move". Use the icon to clarify the verb, never to replace it.

✗ Don't

Stack primaries side-by-side. "Save" and "Submit" should never both be green — secondary the less-common path.

07 · Right rail

The ink rail

A 64px dark column pinned to the right edge. Logo at top, primary destinations below, settings at bottom. The active destination gets a green tick mark.

08 · Mobile

The dark mobile nav pill

A floating dark pill at the bottom of the iOS screen. Active tab uses berry red (not green) on the dark surface — green is too low-contrast there. The "+" sits outside the pill as a separate FAB.
